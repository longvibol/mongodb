==> 

- Take a look at document Piseth : have in Array hobbies have fre: 10 and in file title : fre: 6
- Take a look at document Dara : have in Array hobbies have fre: 5 and in file title : fre: 2


>> db.users.updateMany({"hobbies.fre":{$gte:3}},{$set:{"hobbies.$[e].isNewField":true}},{arrayFilters:[{"e.fre":{$gt:2}}]})
